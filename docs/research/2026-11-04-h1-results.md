## Rules frozen before the run

- **Gaps:** ten unresolved bars were excused exactly; any other gap still blocks. A missing bar cannot signal. A position held through a gap is evaluated from the next observed bar's open.
- **Gap history:** lookback statistics for a gap session use its observed bars, with nothing imputed. Otherwise a one-bar gap would have excluded the stock for 14 sessions.
- **Stress:** every primary trade holding through a registered gap would be marked at -5% net. No trade held through a gap, so the stress equals the base case and did not affect the decision.
- **Splits:** a ticker is ineligible from a split's ex-date until its lookback is wholly post-split. The provider's split reference covered all 46 stocks and returned one split: NFLX, 10-for-1, November 17, 2025. No NFLX trades fall in the first 14 sessions from that date.
- **Decision:** fail if mean daily net return at registered cost is at or below zero or the control's, in the base case or under stress.

## Gap evidence

One-minute aggregate requests returned HTTP 200 for all eight affected sessions, with no one-minute bars inside any of the ten gaps. That is consistent with intervals having no eligible prints, but it does not prove it. Trade-level data remains unavailable on the current plan.

## Limits

Static surviving names flatter a long-only test, so a clean universe would not be expected to rescue this result. Other limits are hypothetical historical availability, assumed costs and fills from bars, and a screen of splits only. The evaluation dates were already exposed in earlier work.

## What this does and does not change

Do not tune H1, and do not buy execution data for it: gross return is already negative. With ORB and H3 also at about zero gross, the cheap bar-only intraday ideas are used up. The remaining choices are H2 on its own economic hypothesis, a registered pairs run, or recording that no edge was found.
